crowsnest.egress#

The page’s sanitiser, made safe against text that an earlier clip has already cut (#83).

Several clips run on a row’s text before the page sees it: a tool call’s argument is cut to 80 characters (crowsnest.activity.describe_tool()), a roster row’s prompt and last words to 240 (crowsnest.tools._clip()), a reason to 200, and the page cuts its own tile lines and raw asks. Each cut keeps a prefix and appends an ellipsis, and a credential or home path cut that way is a shape no pattern in openloops.egress recognises: ghp_AAAA… goes through openloops.dashboard.Sanitizer.text() untouched.

The rows are not changed: their clipped text is attention’s material, so cutting it differently would resurface every item it touches. Instead the one choke point everything reaches the page through, Sanitizer, drops each word an ellipsis ends before it scrubs. A clip always cuts the last word it kept, so that is the only word that can be a fragment. The price is that a word a person wrote before an ellipsis of their own is lost too; a published line saying Let me think… reads Let me …, which loses nothing anyone acts on.

Not for text that this module’s callers clip after sanitising (crowsnest.live.publishable() does): its own ellipsis follows an already-clean word, and dropping that word would eat every clipped line.
